# Assets

`manifest.json` is the source of truth for provenance, license review, redistribution, and required credits.

- `vrm/VRM1_Constraint_Twist_Sample.vrm`: public VRM 1.0 constraint/look-at/spring-bone sample used for the default smoke path.
- `vrm/Seed-san.vrm`: public VRM 1.0 model sample used for model replacement and license-aware metadata checks.
- `vrm/VRMC_vrm_expressions_isBinary_Overridden.vrm` and `vrm/VRMC_vrm_expressions_isBinary_Overrides.vrm`: small public VRM 1.0 expression behavior samples.
- `vrma/test.vrma`: public three-vrm-animation sample used for the default VRMA smoke path.
- `vrma/Relax.vrma`: TK256's MIT-licensed repository-tracked idle candidate; evaluated as a 3.933-second loop.
- `vrma/Goodbye.vrma`: TK256's MIT-licensed repository-tracked one-shot gesture candidate; evaluated as a 3.933-second gesture.
- `vrma/Jump.vrma`: TK256's MIT-licensed repository-tracked larger-motion regression candidate; evaluated as a 3.933-second one-shot.

Vita, Vfg 1.0, Victoria_Rubin, and BOOTH motion packs are recorded as blocked/uncommitted candidates until their current distribution terms can be confirmed without login, consent, or purchase automation.

The three TK256 files were fetched from the pinned `tk256ailab/vrm-viewer` revision recorded in `manifest.json`. The repository's MIT `LICENSE` and file history were checked; no separate file-level restriction was found. Redistribution and credit obligations remain recorded per asset in the manifest.
